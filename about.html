FOUNDATIONAL IDENTITY

A Legacy of Cultural & Artistic Distinction

Established in 1976, Mohammadpur Preparatory School and College has always regarded cultural activities as an important part of its institutional identity. Its annual Cultural Week, featuring singing, dance, poetry recitation, and other creative segments, has been one of the school’s signature traditions since 2003.

Building upon this legacy, the MPSC Cultural Club was established in 2022 across all four wings: Bangla Version Girls, Bangla Version Boys, English Version Girls, and English Version Boys. Since then, the club has hosted a wide range of cultural festivals, workshops, seminars, and creative activities, with each wing contributing to the institution’s growing cultural landscape.

OUR FOUR PILLARS

Unified Across Four Wings

Each wing brings a distinct creative energy and tradition, enriching MPSC’s cultural identity.

Bangla Version Girls

Pioneers in traditional Bengali classical dance, solo recitations, and vibrant cultural celebrations including Modhu Mela 2025 performances.

Bangla Version Boys

Organizers of the CARNIVAL intra-wing festival, instrumental music jams, cultural seminars, and passionate vocal recitals.

English Version Girls

Hosts of The Odyssey of Artistry masterclass (drawing 350+ participants) and Folk Fest 2023 with Bengali character cosplay & quizzes.

English Version Boys

Champions of modern musical ensembles, band performances, collaborative workshops, and artistic seminars.
