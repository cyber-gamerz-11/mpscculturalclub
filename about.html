About MPSC Cultural Club

Founded in 1976, MPSC Cultural Club stands as one of the most prestigious student organizations at Mohammadpur Preparatory School & College. For decades, our club has served as a creative home for students passionate about music, theatre, fine arts, recitation, dance, and cultural heritage.

Our flagship annual festival, The Cultura Fiesta 1.0, brings together talented participants from schools, colleges, and universities across Bangladesh to celebrate creativity, competition, and cultural harmony.
