FOUNDATIONAL IDENTITY

A Legacy of Artistic Excellence

Established in 1976, Mohammadpur Preparatory School and College has always regarded cultural activities as an important part of its institutional identity. Its annual Cultural Week, featuring singing, dance, poetry recitation, and other creative segments, has been one of the school’s signature traditions since 2003.

Building upon this legacy, the MPSC Cultural Club was established in 2022 across all four wings: Bangla Version Girls, Bangla Version Boys, English Version Girls, and English Version Boys. Since then, the club has hosted a wide range of cultural festivals, workshops, seminars, and creative activities, with each wing contributing to the institution’s growing cultural landscape.

JOURNEY THROUGH THE YEARS

Key Institutional Milestones

1976

Institutional Foundation

Mohammadpur Preparatory School and College establishes its core commitment to artistic growth and cultural discovery alongside academic distinction.

2003

Annual Cultural Week Launch

The inauguration of the signature annual Cultural Week tradition, celebrating student talent in solo singing, choreography, poetry recitation, and literature.

2022

MPSC Cultural Club Formation

Unification of all four student wings into a unified student-led organization: Bangla Version Girls, Bangla Version Boys, English Version Girls, and English Version Boys.

2023–2025

Masterclasses & Wing Festivals

Hosting landmark initiatives including The Odyssey of Artistry masterclass with Antik Mahmud, Folk Fest 2023, CARNIVAL, cultural seminars, and Modhu Mela 2025 across all wings.

OCTOBER 1–3, 2026

The Cultura Fiesta 1.0 (National Festival)

Hosting the first national-level cultural festival in the history of Mohammadpur Preparatory School and College, opening doors to students across Bangladesh.

FOUR PILLARS OF OUR CLUB

Explore Our Four Wings

Each wing brings a distinct creative energy, contributing to MPSC’s rich and dynamic cultural landscape.

Bangla Version Girls

BVG WING

Pioneers in traditional Bengali classical dance, solo recitations, and cultural celebrations including Modhu Mela 2025 performances.

Bangla Version Boys

BVB WING

Organizers of CARNIVAL intra-wing festival, instrumental music clashes, cultural seminars, and vocal recitals.

English Version Girls

EVG WING

English Version Boys

EVB WING
